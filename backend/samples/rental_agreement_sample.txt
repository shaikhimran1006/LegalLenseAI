RENTAL / LEASE AGREEMENT (SAMPLE — fictional)

THIS RENTAL AGREEMENT is made on 1 June 2025 between Ms. Priya Deshmukh (the "Landlord") and Mr. Rohan Kulkarni (the "Tenant") for the residential premises at Flat 402, Sunshine Residency, Wakad, Pune.

1. TERM: The tenancy shall be for a fixed term of eleven (11) months, commencing on 1 June 2025.

2. RENT: The monthly rent is Rs. 25,000, payable in advance on or before the 5th day of each month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT: The Tenant shall pay a refundable security deposit of Rs. 1,00,000 (four months' rent). The deposit shall be refunded within 45 days of vacation, less legitimate deductions.

4. RENT ESCALATION: The rent shall be increased by 10% at the time of renewal of this agreement.

5. MAINTENANCE: The Landlord shall attend to structural repairs. The Tenant shall be responsible for day-to-day maintenance and repair of damages caused by the Tenant.

6. DEDUCTIONS: The Landlord may deduct from the security deposit for unpaid rent, damages beyond normal wear and tear, or unpaid utility bills (with itemized statement).

7. NOTICE TO VACATE: Either Party may terminate this agreement by giving sixty (60) days' written notice. Early termination by the Tenant shall be subject to a lock-in period of six (6) months.

8. LOCK-IN: The Tenant shall not vacate within the first six (6) months; on violation, the remaining months' rent shall be payable as liquidated damages.

9. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL: The agreement shall automatically renew for successive eleven-month terms unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before expiry.

10. SUB-LETTING: The Tenant shall not sub-let or part with possession of the premises without prior written consent of the Landlord.

11. UTILITIES: Electricity, water and internet charges shall be paid by the Tenant directly to the respective providers.

12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION: Disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved by mutual discussion and, failing which, through the competent courts at Pune only.
