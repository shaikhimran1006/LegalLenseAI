EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (SAMPLE — fictional)

THIS EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is entered into on 1 April 2025 by and between Acme Technologies Private Limited (the "Company") and Mr. Alex Sharma (the "Employee").

1. POSITION: Senior Software Engineer, reporting to the Head of Engineering.

2. PROBATION: The Employee shall be on probation for a period of six (6) months. During probation, either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving seven (7) days' written notice.

3. COMPENSATION: The Company shall pay the Employee a monthly basic salary of Rs. 85,000 plus a monthly special allowance of Rs. 15,000, subject to applicable tax deductions.

4. NOTICE OF RESIGNATION: The Employee shall provide no less than thirty (30) days' written notice of resignation, or payment in lieu at the Company's discretion. Release from notice requires written waiver.

5. TERMINATION FOR CAUSE: The Company may terminate for cause (breach, misconduct, fraud or acts detrimental to the Company's interests) effective immediately upon written notice, without notice or pay in lieu.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY: The Employee shall keep confidential all proprietary and confidential information, including trade secrets, client data, business plans and financial information, during and after employment.

7. NON-SOLICITATION: For twelve (12) months after termination, the Employee shall not solicit clients or employees of the Company.

8. TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT: 8.1 The Company may sponsor external training programs. 8.2 Training costs: Should the Employee resign within eighteen (18) months of completion of a sponsored training program worth Rs. 50,000, the Employee shall repay the entire training cost within one month of resignation, with interest at 18% per annum on delay.

9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY: All work created during employment is work made for hire and all intellectual property rights vest in the Company.

10. NON-COMPETITION: For twelve (12) months following termination, the Employee shall not engage in any business that competes with the Company within a radius of fifty (50) kilometers.

11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION: Disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated in Pune, Maharashtra. The award shall be final and binding.

12. LEAVE AND BENEFITS: Fifteen (15) days of paid annual leave and medical insurance coverage per Company policy.

13. GENERAL: This Agreement is governed by the laws of India and constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.
